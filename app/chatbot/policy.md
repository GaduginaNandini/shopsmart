## Shipping
ShopSmart ships orders within 2 business days of payment. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days. Shipping is free on orders above 999 INR.

## Order status
Order statuses are PAID, PACKED, SHIPPED, DELIVERED and CANCELLED. You can ask the chatbot for the status of an order by giving the order number.

## Refund policy
Refunds are available within 7 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refund approval is decided by a human support agent.

## Cancellation policy
An order can be cancelled before it is shipped. Once an order is SHIPPED it cannot be cancelled; use the refund policy after delivery.

## Payment
ShopSmart accepts card payments. This demo uses a mocked payment gateway and never stores card details.

## Coupons
Coupon SAVE10 gives 10 percent off the cart. Bulk purchases of 10 or more units of one product get 5 percent off that item.

## Contact
For anything not covered here, a human support agent will reply by email within 1 business day.
